# Eldor Explains: money research brief (as of 2026-11-17)

Copy of the research doc made in the "Automated AI YouTube project" chat. Live doc (may hold later edits):
https://claude.ai/artifact/LP1C4SshvhaHmyu2JtoX9f

Bottom line: the money is in YouTube long-form plus sponsorships, not in short-form payouts. An automated channel
only gets paid if each video carries original analysis and a real human signal (named host, own voice).

## What each platform pays (creator share per 1,000 views)

| Platform | Pays | Entry bar | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube long-form | ~$2.30 all-niche median; $10.22 education median | 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours (8,000 for new channels from 2027-02-01) | 8+ min videos with mid-rolls; the main engine |
| TikTok Creator Rewards | $0.40-1.00 (US ~$0.85) | 10,000 followers + 100,000 views in 30 days | Only videos over 1 minute earn |
| YouTube Shorts | $0.03-0.30 (finance up to ~$1.50) | 1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views in 90 days | Funnel to long-form |
| Facebook Content Monetization | $0.02-0.20 on Reels | Invite-only | Paid ~$3B to creators in 2025 |
| Instagram | No reliable per-view pay | Invite-only bonuses | Brand deals and affiliates |

Sponsorships: $15-80 per 1,000 views, finance at the top. Past ~50k monthly views, sponsors and affiliates usually
out-earn ads.

## Which niches pay

- AIR Media-Tech 2026 (300 channels, 3,595 channel-months): Education & Science median RPM $10.22 (middle half
  $2.31-19.50); Business & Finance middle half only $0.73-5.17. The "$20-45 finance RPM" figures online come mostly
  from AI-tool vendor blogs.
- Spread inside a niche is wider than between niches. What moves a channel up: US/UK audience, 8+ minute videos,
  advertiser-friendly topics.
- Takeaway: an educational "how money works" channel gets education RPMs and still attracts finance sponsors.

## YouTube rules that decide whether automation gets paid

July 2026 clarification of the "inauthentic content" policy (judged channel-wide), three non-monetizable categories:

1. Generic or repetitive: template videos, slideshows, AI content without the creator's own perspective.
   -> Every episode gets its own dataset, angle and structure.
2. Unsatisfying or off-putting: shock formulas, AI clips with no logical flow, deceptive fake footage.
   -> Clear narrative, no fake realistic footage.
3. AI personas on sensitive topics: an AI presented as a human expert giving advice on health, legal, money or politics.
   -> Never an AI "finance expert". Explain, never advise. A real named person (Eldor) fronts the channel.

Still monetizable: AI visuals/avatars that don't pose as experts, creator insight, varied videos with a story.
A plain AI voiceover of original material generally needs no AI label; cloning your own voice is fine.

YPP timing: new channels applying after 2027-02-01 need 8,000 watch hours (~22 hours a day for a year) or
20M Shorts views. Fan funding still opens at 500 subs + 3,000 hours.

## Revenue math, year one (planning assumptions, not sourced)

Assumptions: one 8-12 min video a week, mostly US/UK audience, $6 RPM once monetized, ~4 min watched per view
(8,000 hours ~ 120,000 views).

| Scenario | Monthly views at month 12 | Ads | Sponsors | Total/month at month 12 | Hits 8,000 hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slow | 20,000 | $0 | $0-300 | ~$0-300 | Month 15-18 |
| Base | 100,000 | ~$600 | ~$600-1,200 | ~$1,200-1,800 | Month 9-12 |
| One breakout | 500,000 | ~$3,000 | ~$3,000+ | ~$6,000+ | Month 4-6 |

Running costs should stay under ~$150/month, mostly shared with Bilmasboy.

## Recommended model (adopted)

- Pillars: "The Numbers Behind It" (one public dataset per episode), "The World You Don't Hear About" (Central Asia /
  post-Soviet explained for Western viewers, monthly), "How X actually makes money" (format validated by Marcus
  Explains: <1 year old, ~86k average views, a 465k-view breakout).
- Pipeline: topic scoring -> data pull and draft -> 15-minute owner gate (later changed: Claude approves English
  scripts) -> production (voice clone, chart scenes, AI visuals) -> distribution (TikTok cuts just over 1 minute,
  Shorts under 60 s pointing to the long video, Facebook Reels).
- Income order: affiliates from month 1 -> TikTok Creator Rewards at 10k followers -> sponsors at ~5-10k views per
  video -> YouTube ads after the 8,000-hour bar -> memberships / data product later.
- 90-day checkpoint: keep going if average view percentage >= 40%, thumbnail CTR >= 5%, and at least one video passes
  10k views. Otherwise change topics, not format.
- Faster-cash side option raised (not decided): sell the engine's output as explainer videos for businesses
  (~3 clients x $1,500/month).
