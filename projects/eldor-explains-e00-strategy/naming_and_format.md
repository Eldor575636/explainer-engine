# Eldor Explains: naming and on-screen format (decided 2026-11-17)

Setup work from the "Automated AI YouTube project" chat. No scripts, voice, pictures or code were made in that chat;
episode work starts in projects/ep06-gold/en/.

## Channel name: Eldor Explains (owner pick)

Owner's reasons: personal, the mascot is Eldor anyway, no plan to sell the channel.

Host name checks:
- Eldor: chosen. Easy to say, searchable, matches the real About page (real-person signal).
- El: too generic (Spanish "the"), not ownable. Fine as an in-video nickname.
- Al: rejected. Reads as "AI" in most fonts, bad for an AI-produced channel.

El Dorado: used as backstory and visual detail (gold item on the mascot, possible origin episode "The City of Gold
That Never Existed"), not as the brand (Cadillac/casinos/trademark crowding).

Names considered and dropped (web search 2026-11-17):
- The Golden Question: existing politics/economics podcast (2020-2024).
- Follow the Coin: existing crypto channel.
- "Ledger" names (Dark Ledger, Magnified Ledger), "Hidden ___" (The Hidden Cost, The Price of Ownership, The Invisible
  Hand), "Golden ___" (many channels), "Money ___" (How Money Works, Money & Macro): crowded.
- True Worth: too close to BuzzFeed's "Worth It".
- El Explaino: unused, but reads as mock Spanish, may pull Spanish-speaking (lower RPM) traffic, no money signal.
- Runners-up kept on file: Worth Knowing, The Prospector.

## On-screen format

- No filming at all (owner, 2026-11-17: filming is a waste of his time).
- Cartoon likeness of Eldor + his ElevenLabs voice clone; lip-sync driven from the voice audio for host shots.
- Mascot built once (character sheet from 15-20 photos, then a 25-30 pose library on transparent backgrounds);
  the engine picks poses by script emotion/action tags. Three roles: host (corner/half screen), reactor (beside charts),
  in-scene (custom pictures with the mascot).
- When the narration is about a specific real person, that person is on screen; the mascot only when a line has no
  main character (same rule as Bilmasboy).
- Real-person signal without filming: real name, photo and finance background on the About page and in descriptions;
  optional real photo in banner/end screen.
